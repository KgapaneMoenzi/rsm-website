Questions

Good to Know

How much does a room cost?

Single and Bachelor rooms are R4 500 per month. Sharing rooms (2 students) are R3 800 per month. Transport to UMP, Wi-Fi and the kitchen are included.

Do you accept NSFAS and bursary students?

Yes. We accommodate NSFAS beneficiaries, students on bursaries, and self-funded students.

How does transport to campus work?

Residents have access to 9 daily trips to the University of Mpumalanga: 4 in the morning to campus and 5 return trips in the afternoon/evening.

What is included in every kitchen?

Each kitchen has a 4-plate stove, a refrigerator and a microwave, ready for use.

Who will I share a bathroom and kitchen with?

In Single and Sharing rooms, the shower and kitchen are shared with only one other person. Bachelor rooms are fully private and en-suite.

How long until I hear back after applying?

We aim to review your application and contact you within 24 hours.
